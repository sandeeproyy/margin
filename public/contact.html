02

What to include.

Account help

The email address on the account and what you were trying to do. Never send your password or Razorpay credentials.

Print help

The date of the job, the page count, and what happened. A photo of the affected pages helps with a print-quality problem. Please do not attach the document unless we ask for it.

Payment or refund help

The Razorpay payment ID (starts with pay_), the order ID if you have it (starts with order_), the amount, and the date. Do not send card numbers, UPI PINs, or bank passwords.

Privacy request

Write from the account email and tell us whether you want to see, correct, or delete your data. We may ask a small question to verify the request before acting on it.

03

Payments & Razorpay.

Payments are processed by Razorpay Software Private Limited. Margin never receives your full card, UPI, or bank credentials. If your bank statement shows a Razorpay entry, the payment ID from your confirmation will help us match it.

For a refund request, include the payment ID and read the Refunds & cancellations page first. For pricing, see Pricing.
