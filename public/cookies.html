01

The short version.

Margin uses one essential cookie so a signed-in session survives a page reload. It does not use analytics, advertising, social-media pixels, or cross-site tracking cookies. No cookie is used to build a profile of you.

Because the sign-in cookie is strictly necessary to provide the service you asked for, it is always on. There is no optional tracking to consent to. The notice simply makes that clear and lets you review the details.

02

What cookies are.

A cookie is a small text file a site asks your browser to keep. It can remember that you are signed in, keep a preference, or help a payment provider complete a transaction. Browser storage — local storage and session storage — works in a similar way but stays inside the browser.

First-party cookies belong to the site you are visiting. Third-party cookies are set by another service loaded into the page, such as a payment provider. Margin itself sets only a first-party sign-in cookie.

The session cookie contains a signed session reference, not your password or document. It is marked Secure on HTTPS, cannot be read by page scripts, and is removed when you sign out or when the account is disabled, reset, or deleted.

If you block this cookie, you cannot sign in or use the print room. Other sites cannot read it.

04

Browser storage.

  • Printer choice: local storage remembers which listed printer you picked, so the sheet opens with the same one next time.
  • Active job: session storage keeps the current job reference while the tab is open, so a reload does not lose the status screen.
  • Cookie choice: local storage remembers that you have seen this notice, so it does not appear on every visit.

These values stay on your device. They are not sent to advertisers and are not used to track you across other websites. Clearing site data in your browser removes them.

05

Payments & Razorpay.

Nothing from Razorpay loads until you choose to pay for a print. At that point, Razorpay Checkout may set its own cookies or similar storage to keep the payment session together, apply the amount, and prevent fraud. Those cookies are controlled by Razorpay, not Margin.

Razorpay processes card, UPI, and bank details under its own privacy policy. Margin receives only the order and payment identifiers needed to verify that the payment succeeded. It never receives your full card number, UPI PIN, or bank password.

Read Razorpay’s privacy policy for the details of its cookies and processing.

06

Your choice.

You can clear or block cookies in your browser at any time. Blocking the sign-in cookie signs you out and prevents printing; the rest of the public policy pages will still load. You can also open the cookie panel above and save an essential-only choice.

Margin does not use a consent banner to unlock advertising cookies, because there are none. The panel exists so the position is explicit and easy to inspect. If optional cookies are ever introduced, they will be off until you turn them on.

For a privacy request about account data, see the Privacy notice and the Contact page.
