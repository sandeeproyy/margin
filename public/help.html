01

Signing in.

Margin is a private print room. The owner invites each person individually, so there is no public sign-up. Use the email address and password you chose when you accepted the invitation.

If you have an invitation code but no account yet, choose Create account on the sign-in screen, enter the code, and pick a password of at least ten characters. The code works once.

↳

Keep your account to yourself. Your files and printing history belong to your account. The owner cannot open them, and neither can another member.

02

Sending a document.

From the main room, choose a file or drop it onto the paper card. PDFs, images, text files, and Word, Excel, or PowerPoint documents are welcome. The size limit is shown under the card.

The file travels straight to the laptop that owns the printer. It is prepared there, and a preview appears in a moment. You can close the tab after the job is sent; the laptop keeps working.

!

Office files need desktop Office. If a Word, Excel, or PowerPoint file will not prepare, open it in Office and save it as a PDF, then send the PDF instead.

03

Checking the pages.

Before sending anything to paper, Margin shows the document's pages and a few sensible settings. Look over the preview, then choose:

PagesAll, or a range
CopiesOne, or a few
PaperSize and source
InkColor or black

The print sheet only offers what the chosen printer can actually do. If a page looks blank in the original, Margin points it out so you can decide whether to print it.

04

The PIN and print.

The first time you print, Margin asks for the print room PIN. The owner shares this separately. It is a second lock in front of the physical printer, so a stray click or an unknown visitor cannot make paper move.

Every physical page is ₹1. There is no free page. The exact amount appears before you pay, and Razorpay handles the payment. Nothing is charged until you choose to print.

  1. 1
    Enter the PIN.

    Type the number the owner gave you. It stays unlocked for your session for about thirty minutes.

  2. 2
    Review the summary.

    Check the page count, copies, paper, and orientation. Choose Print when it looks right.

  3. 3
    Wait for the tray.

    The status screen follows the job from preparation to the printer. A sent job means the laptop accepted it; paper and ink still need to cooperate.

↳

One job at a time. If the room is busy, give the current job a moment. The owner can see printer status, but never your document.

05

Privacy & limits.

Margin keeps documents out of cloud storage. Your file goes to the laptop, is prepared for the printer, and is removed automatically after a couple of hours. Your history keeps the file name, the settings, and the result — not the document itself.

Your historyOnly you can see it
The ownerAccounts, not documents
Other membersSeparate rooms
Daily allowanceShown in your account

Each member has a daily page allowance and a limit on active jobs. The allowance resets each day. If you run out, the message will tell you when to try again.

06

When something sticks.

The printer shows as unavailable.

The laptop may be asleep, offline, or between printer choices. Ask the owner to check that the laptop is awake and the printer is switched on and connected. Nothing will print on a different printer by surprise.

Nothing comes out, but the app says sent.

The app confirms that the laptop accepted the job, not that ink met paper. Ask the owner to check the printer queue for a paused or stuck job, then check paper, ink, and the cable.

I forgot the print PIN.

Ask the print room owner. The PIN is shared separately from your account password, and it is not shown anywhere in the app.

My invitation code does not work.

Invitation codes work once and expire. Ask the owner for a fresh one. If you already created an account, use Sign in instead.

Can the owner read my documents?

No. The owner manages accounts and chooses which printer is available, but every document and history entry is scoped to the person who uploaded it. Margin never shows another member's file to the owner.

How long is my file kept?

About two hours after upload, or sooner if the room is cleaned up. The history entry stays small: name, pages, settings, and whether the job was sent. You can upload the file again if you need another copy.

How much does printing cost?

Every physical page is ₹1. There is no free page. You pay per print job through Razorpay, not from a prepaid balance. The amount is shown before the payment window opens. See Pricing for examples.

Can I print the same document twice?

A prepared job prints once. If you need another copy, upload it again. For multiple copies in one go, use the copies control before printing.
