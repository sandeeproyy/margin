01

What a job costs.

Every physical page is priced at ₹1. This applies to every account, including the room owner. A paid job is never less than ₹1, because that is the smallest charge Razorpay can accept in Indian rupees.

Copies multiply the page count. Two copies of a three-page document count as six pages. The exact amount is always shown before you pay.

02

When you pay.

Margin charges per print job, at the moment you choose to print. There is no prepaid balance and no stored credit to spend later.

  1. 1
    Review the quote.

    The print sheet shows the page count and the exact amount before any payment opens.

  2. 2
    Pay through Razorpay.

    Razorpay handles the payment page. Card, UPI, and bank details are entered there, never on Margin.

  3. 3
    The print is released.

    Margin verifies the payment with Razorpay before sending the job to the printer. A failed or unverified payment does not release the print.

Payments are processed by Razorpay Software Private Limited. Their terms and privacy policy also apply to the checkout. Margin receives the order and payment identifiers, the amount, and the result, but not your full card or bank credentials.

03

Holds & cancellation.

Starting a checkout keeps that order open for 30 minutes. While it is open, a checkout for a different document waits. If you cancel the checkout or it expires, the order is released and nothing is charged.

You can close the Razorpay window without paying. No money moves until Razorpay confirms a successful payment. If the page count or price changes while a checkout is open, Margin rejects the old quote rather than charging a different amount.

04

If printing fails.

If a payment succeeds but the print cannot be delivered because of a fault on our side, you can ask for a reprint or a refund. The same applies to a duplicate charge or a charge for a job that never reached the printer.

If the document, page range, paper size, or copies were chosen incorrectly, the print is not refundable. Please check the preview and summary before paying. The full rules are on the Refunds & cancellations page.

05

Taxes & changes.

All amounts are in Indian rupees. The amount shown at checkout is the amount charged. Margin is operated by an individual in India.

The per-page price or minimum charge may change. A change never alters a quote you have already paid. The price shown in your print sheet is the price that applies to that job.
