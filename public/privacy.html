01

Who is responsible.

Margin is operated by Sandeep Roy, an individual in India. For the limited personal data described here, the operator is the data controller.

Margin is invitation only. It is not a public document-hosting service, and it is not designed for sensitive records such as medical files, financial account numbers, or government identifiers. Please do not send those unless you are comfortable printing them through this room.

02

What we collect.

Account details

Your name, email address, password hash, account role, account status, and dates such as when the account was created or last used. We never store your password in plain text.

Print-job details

The file name, page count, print settings, job status, timestamps, and the temporary document and preview files needed to prepare the print. Your history shows the job details, not the document itself.

Payment details

Razorpay order and payment identifiers, the amount and currency, the page count, and the payment result. Margin does not receive or store your full card number, UPI PIN, or bank credentials.

Technical details

The service uses your IP address in short-lived, in-memory counters to limit sign-in attempts and abusive requests. The server may keep operational error logs. The browser stores a printer choice and the state of an active job; the session itself is held in a secure cookie.

03

Where documents go.

When you send a file, the browser uploads it directly to the laptop that owns the printer through a private connection. The public website is static; it does not store your document. The laptop converts the file, prepares previews, and sends the finished job to the chosen printer.

Temporary files are removed automatically, normally within about two hours, or sooner when the room is cleaned up. A preview may remain on the laptop only for as long as the job is active. Your history keeps the file name, page count, settings, and result, but not the document contents.

↳

One exception to know. The owner controls the laptop’s operating system. The Margin interface does not show one member’s files to another member or to the owner, but someone with administrator access to the laptop could reach local files while they still exist. That is why files are deleted automatically and should not be treated as permanent storage.

04

Why we use it.

  • To run the room: sign you in, prepare documents, apply your page allowance, and send a job to the printer.
  • To take payment: create a Razorpay order, verify the result, and keep a record of the transaction.
  • To keep it safe: rate-limit attempts, investigate errors, prevent abuse, and protect other members.
  • To help you: respond when you ask about an account, a print, or a charge.
  • To meet the law: keep records where required and respond to valid legal requests.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or build behavioural profiles. Margin does not run third-party analytics on the print room.

05

Cookies & browser storage.

Margin sets one essential session cookie so you stay signed in. It is HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, and it lasts for about 30 days unless you sign out or the account is changed.

The browser also uses local storage to remember your printer choice and your cookie choice, and session storage to remember an active job while the tab is open. These are not advertising trackers. The Cookie policy lists each item and lets you reopen the cookie panel.

When you open Razorpay Checkout, Razorpay may set its own cookies or similar storage to process the payment and prevent fraud. Those are governed by Razorpay’s privacy policy.

06

Who we share with.

  • Razorpay processes payments and receives the amount, order details, and the information needed to complete your payment.
  • Vercel serves the static public pages and routes API requests. It may process standard request metadata such as IP addresses and user agents.
  • Cloudflare provides the private network connection used to reach the laptop. It may process connection metadata.
  • Authorities or advisors may receive information when required by law, or when needed to establish or defend a legal claim.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share your documents with other members, advertisers, or the public. A payment provider may process data outside India under its own safeguards.

08

How it is protected.

Passwords are hashed with scrypt. Sessions use signed, HttpOnly cookies. The printer connection is kept behind a private tunnel, and every job and history entry is scoped to the account that created it. Sign-in, registration, uploads, and prints are rate-limited. API responses are marked no-store, and the site enforces a strict Content Security Policy and origin checks.

The owner can manage accounts without seeing member email addresses or opening anyone’s documents. Print history is private to each member, and the owner dashboard shows only aggregate earnings and recent payment amounts.

No system is perfect. If you believe an account or a document has been exposed, email us immediately so we can investigate and, where needed, end sessions or rotate access.

09

Your choices.

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal data linked to your account. You can also ask us to stop using it, subject to records we must keep by law. Email sandeeproy4984@gmail.com from the address on the account so we can verify the request.

You can sign out at any time, clear the browser’s local storage, and ask the owner to close your account. You can review or reopen the cookie panel from any page footer or the Cookie policy. Deleting the app’s browser data does not delete files already prepared on the laptop; those are removed by the normal cleanup.

Margin is not intended for children. You must be at least 18 to create an account.
