A fair reprint.

A print job is a small, made-to-order service. If a paid job fails because of something on our side, we will reprint it or refund it. If the document or settings were chosen incorrectly, the print is not refundable.

01

Cancelling before print.

You can close the Razorpay checkout without paying. No money moves until Razorpay confirms a successful payment, so closing the window costs nothing.

Starting a checkout keeps the order open for 30 minutes. Cancelling the checkout or letting it expire releases the order. If a print has already been sent to the printer, it cannot be recalled through Margin; contact us if something went wrong.

02

When we refund.

We will review a refund when a paid job is affected by a fault on our side, including:

  • payment succeeded, but no print job was created or released;
  • a duplicate charge for the same job;
  • the amount charged does not match the quote shown before payment;
  • the printer was unavailable or the job failed before any paper was printed;
  • a printer fault stopped the job partway and the remaining pages cannot be delivered.

Where a partial job was delivered, we may refund the undelivered portion or reprint the missing pages rather than refund the whole amount.

03

When we don’t.

A print is made specifically for you once it is released. We do not refund a completed job because:

  • the wrong document, page range, paper size, colour mode, or number of copies was chosen;
  • blank pages shown in the preview were included in the job;
  • the printed result differs slightly from the screen, or paper, ink, or the printer produced a normal physical imperfection;
  • you changed your mind after the job was printed.

If you are unsure, stop before paying and check the preview and summary. The print sheet is designed to show exactly what will be sent.

04

Reprint first.

For a fault on our side, a reprint is usually the fastest fix. If the same document and settings will solve it, we can release a replacement print at no extra charge. If a reprint is not useful, choose a refund instead.

We may ask for a photo of the affected pages or a short description so we can check the printer. Please do not email the document itself unless we specifically ask for it.

05

How to ask.

Email sandeeproy4984@gmail.com within seven days of the payment. Include:

  1. 1
    The Razorpay payment ID.

    It starts with pay_ and appears in the payment confirmation. The order ID, starting with order_, also helps.

  2. 2
    The account email and date.

    Use the address on your Margin account and the date of the charge.

  3. 3
    What went wrong.

    Tell us whether the job did not start, stopped partway, duplicated, or showed a different amount. A photo helps for a print-quality problem.

06

Refund timing.

We aim to reply within two business days. If a refund is approved, we start it through Razorpay within two business days of approval. Razorpay normally takes 5–7 business days to return the money to the original payment method; your bank or UPI provider may take a little longer.

Refunds go back to the method used for the payment. We cannot send a refund to a different card, account, or person.

07

Before a chargeback.

If a charge looks wrong, please email us first. Most payment problems can be fixed faster with the payment ID than through a bank dispute. If you do raise a chargeback, we may share the order and payment records with Razorpay and the bank to respond to it.
